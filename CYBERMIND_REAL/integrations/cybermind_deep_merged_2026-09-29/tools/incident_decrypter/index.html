OFFLINE DECRYPTER

Decrypt an incident brief

Open a CYBERMIND encrypted JSON report with the key sent to you separately. Decryption runs on this device; this tool makes no network requests and does not need the model or database.

New reports use a unique 256-bit key. Older passphrase-protected reports remain supported. The key is not included in the encrypted file.

Readable incident brief

The downloaded readable copy is not encrypted. Store and share it accordingly.
